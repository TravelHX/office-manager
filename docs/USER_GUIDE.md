# Office Manager User Guide

## Introduction

Welcome to Office Manager! This guide will help you use the application to manage your desk bookings, parking reservations, and overtime tracking.

## Getting Started

### Accessing the Application

1. Open your web browser
2. Navigate to the Office Manager URL (provided by your administrator)
3. The application will automatically authenticate you

### Navigation

The main navigation menu is located at the top of every page:

- **Home** - Dashboard with summary statistics
- **Desk Booking** - Book and manage desk reservations
- **Parking** - Reserve parking spaces
- **Overtime** - Record and track overtime hours
- **My Bookings** - View all your bookings, reservations, and overtime records
- **Admin** - Administrative functions (admin users only)

## Desk Booking

### Booking a Desk

1. Click on **Desk Booking** in the navigation menu
2. Select your desired start date and end date
3. Click **Check Availability** to see available desks
4. Review the list of available desks
5. Click **Book This Desk** on your preferred desk
6. Confirm your booking

### Viewing Your Bookings

1. Click on **My Bookings** in the navigation menu
2. Your active desk bookings will be displayed in the "Desk Bookings" section
3. Each booking shows:
   - Desk number and location
   - Start and end dates
   - Status (active or cancelled)

### Cancelling a Booking

1. Navigate to **My Bookings**
2. Find the booking you want to cancel
3. Click the **Cancel** button
4. Confirm the cancellation

**Note:** Once cancelled, the desk becomes immediately available for other users.

## Parking Reservations

### Reserving a Parking Space

1. Click on **Parking** in the navigation menu
2. Select the date you need parking
3. Choose a time period:
   - **Morning** - Typically 8 AM to 12 PM
   - **Afternoon** - Typically 1 PM to 5 PM
   - **Full Day** - Typically 8 AM to 5 PM
4. Click **Check Availability** to see available parking spaces
5. Review the list of available spaces
6. Click **Reserve This Space** on your preferred parking space
7. Confirm your reservation

### Viewing Your Reservations

1. Click on **My Bookings** in the navigation menu
2. Your active parking reservations will be displayed in the "Parking Reservations" section
3. Each reservation shows:
   - Parking space number and location
   - Reservation date
   - Time period (morning, afternoon, or full day)
   - Status (active or cancelled)

### Cancelling a Reservation

1. Navigate to **My Bookings**
2. Find the reservation you want to cancel
3. Click the **Cancel** button
4. Confirm the cancellation

## Overtime Tracking

### Recording Overtime

1. Click on **Overtime** in the navigation menu
2. Fill in the overtime form:
   - **Date** - The date you worked overtime
   - **Start Time** - When you started working overtime (e.g., 17:00)
   - **End Time** - When you finished working overtime (e.g., 18:00)
   - **Description** - Optional description of work performed
3. The total hours will be calculated automatically
4. Click **Record Overtime** to save

**Note:** You can only record overtime for past dates, not future dates.

### Viewing Overtime History

1. Click on **My Bookings** in the navigation menu
2. Your overtime records will be displayed in the "Overtime Records" section
3. Each record shows:
   - Date
   - Start and end times
   - Total hours
   - Description
   - Status (pending, approved, or rejected)

### Editing Overtime Records

1. Navigate to **Overtime** page
2. View your overtime history
3. Click **Edit** on the record you want to modify
4. Update the details
5. Save your changes

**Note:** You can only edit records with "pending" status.

## My Bookings Page

The **My Bookings** page provides a unified view of all your reservations and records:

### Features

- **Search** - Search across all bookings, reservations, and overtime records
- **Filter by Status** - Filter by active, cancelled, pending, or approved
- **Filter by Type** - Filter by desk bookings, parking reservations, or overtime records
- **Quick Actions** - Cancel bookings and reservations directly from the list

### Using Search and Filters

1. Enter search terms in the search box to find specific items
2. Use the **Status** dropdown to filter by status
3. Use the **Type** dropdown to filter by item type
4. Filters can be combined for more specific results

## Dashboard

The **Home** page provides a quick overview:

- **Active Desk Bookings** - Number of upcoming desk bookings
- **Parking Reservations** - Number of active parking reservations
- **Overtime Hours** - Total approved overtime hours for the current month
- **Upcoming Items** - List of your next 5 upcoming bookings and reservations

## Tips and Best Practices

1. **Plan Ahead** - Book desks and parking spaces in advance to ensure availability
2. **Check Availability** - Always check availability before making bookings
3. **Cancel Early** - Cancel bookings you no longer need so others can use them
4. **Record Overtime Promptly** - Record overtime hours as soon as possible after working
5. **Use Search** - Use the search and filter features to quickly find specific bookings

## Troubleshooting

### I can't see any available desks/parking spaces

- Check that you've selected valid dates (not in the past)
- Try different dates or time periods
- The resource may be fully booked for your selected dates

### I can't cancel my booking

- Only active bookings can be cancelled
- If you see "Cancelled" status, the booking has already been cancelled
- Contact an administrator if you need assistance

### My overtime record shows "pending" status

- Overtime records require approval from an administrator
- Pending records can be edited or deleted
- Once approved or rejected, records cannot be modified

### I'm getting authentication errors

- Make sure you're accessing the application from the correct URL
- Contact your administrator if authentication issues persist

## Admin Features

If you have admin privileges, you can access additional features:

### Resource Configuration

1. Navigate to **Admin** page
2. Click on **Resource Configuration** tab
3. Update desk count or parking count
4. Click **Save Configuration**

**Note:** You cannot reduce counts below the number of active bookings/reservations.

### Viewing All Bookings

1. Navigate to **Admin** page
2. Click on **All Bookings** tab
3. View all desk bookings across all users
4. Cancel any booking with a reason if needed

### Managing Parking Reservations

1. Navigate to **Admin** page
2. Click on **All Parking Reservations** tab
3. View all parking reservations across all users
4. Cancel any reservation with a reason if needed

### Viewing Overtime Records

1. Navigate to **Admin** page
2. Click on **All Overtime Records** tab
3. View all overtime records across all users
4. Review and approve/reject overtime records
